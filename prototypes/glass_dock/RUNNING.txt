Glass Dock prototype (Windows)

A column of glass cards on the right edge of the desktop. Each card shows a
summary and grows into its detail while the pointer rests on it. The dock
reserves no screen space, sits behind other windows by default, and lets
clicks outside the cards pass through to the desktop.

Cards: AirPods (battery, connect, switch output), media, volume, output
device, quick launch. Drop apps, shortcuts or folders on the quick-launch
card to add them; right-click a tile twice to remove it.

Pointer near the dock swells the nearest card. Resting on a card opens its
detail in a flyout beside the dock; the cards never grow over each other, so
the rest stay reachable. Moving to another card slides the flyout there after
a short pause (none if the pointer reaches the flyout first). Leaving both
closes it. Quick-launch tiles swell toward the pointer, show their name in a
bubble and hop when launched.

Hover the dock to show a grip above it; drag the grip to move the dock up and
down its edge. The position is saved (settings > 화면 > 세로 위치 resets it).

Minimized (tray: 최소화 (레일)) the dock is a rail of icons with status dots;
hovering widens it into labels, clicking a row opens its detail in the flyout.

An island grows out of a black pill at the top of the screen for 4 s when the
AirPods connect, disconnect, or a bud/case drops to 20% or lower (once until it
charges or rises above 25%). It never takes clicks. --demo cycles these events
every 6 s; --capture writes dock-*.png and island-*.png frames.

Settings window: left-click the tray icon, or right-click > 설정 열기. Changes
apply at once and are saved in dock.ini under the app's local data folder.
  화면    monitor map; which screen and edge (left/right) the dock sits on
          ("자동" = the outermost monitor on that edge, so the dock is never
          on the seam between two monitors), identify screens, rail mode,
          always on top
  모듈    which cards show and their order (volume and output share a row
          when next to each other), quick-launch apps (add, remove)
  알림    island screen(s) on a map (primary, dock's screen, all, under the
          cursor, or one picked on the map), alignment, top margin, events,
          hide during games, preview
  AirPods device, battery reading (automatic or chosen sources), low-battery
          threshold and warning sound, in-ear time
  미디어  follow whatever plays, pause/resume on ear removal
  일반    start with Windows, quit
The tray menu keeps quick switches: settings, rail, always on top, island
preview, quit.

The real dock reuses the glass widget's saved AirPods, battery sources, output
and media choices (%LOCALAPPDATA%\GlassAudioWidget). In-ear time is shared with
the old widget (%LOCALAPPDATA%\AirPodsWidget\usage.json).

The warning sound is assets\low_power_warning.mp3, which you supply (README);
without it the dock stays silent.

Windows app: .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m PyInstaller --clean --noconfirm GlassDock.spec
then run dist\GlassDock\GlassDock.exe (keep the _internal folder beside it).

Run from the repository root in PowerShell, using the existing environment:

  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py --demo      (sample data)
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py --on-top    (above other windows)

Preview PNGs without a desktop (sample data, stand-in wallpaper):

  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py --capture design-previews\glass-dock

Icons are placed by their optical center (optical.py): the ink's bounding-box
center blended with its alpha centroid, not the 24x24 canvas center.

Checked on Windows 10: click-through outside the cards and Win+D both work.
Acrylic needs Windows 11 22H2+; on Windows 10 the cards use the fallback tint.
Not yet checked on Windows 11.
