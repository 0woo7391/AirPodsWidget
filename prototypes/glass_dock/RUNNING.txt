Glass Dock prototype (Windows)

A column of glass cards on the right edge of the desktop. Each card shows a
summary and grows into its detail while the pointer rests on it. The dock
reserves no screen space, sits behind other windows by default, and lets
clicks outside the cards pass through to the desktop.

Cards: AirPods (battery, connect, switch output), media, volume, output
device, quick launch. Drop apps, shortcuts or folders on the quick-launch
card to add them; right-click a tile twice to remove it.

Pointer near the dock swells the nearest card; resting on a card unfolds it
(spring) into its detail. Quick-launch tiles swell toward the pointer, show
their name in a bubble and hop when launched.

Minimized (tray: 최소화 (레일)) the dock is a rail of icons with status dots;
hovering widens it into labels, clicking a row opens its detail.

An island grows out of a black pill at the top of the screen for 4 s when the
AirPods connect, disconnect, or a bud/case drops to 20% or lower (once until it
charges or rises above 25%). It never takes clicks. --demo cycles these events
every 6 s; --capture writes dock-*.png and island-*.png frames.

Tray settings (saved in dock.ini under the app's local data folder): always on
top, minimized rail, which screen the dock sits on, and for the island: which
screen(s) (primary, secondary, all, under the cursor), alignment, top margin,
which events, and a preview.

Run from the repository root in PowerShell, using the existing environment:

  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py --demo      (sample data)
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py --on-top    (above other windows)

Preview PNGs without a desktop (sample data, stand-in wallpaper):

  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe prototypes\glass_dock\dock.py --capture design-previews\glass-dock

Icons are placed by their optical center (optical.py): the ink's bounding-box
center blended with its alpha centroid, not the 24x24 canvas center.

Checked on Windows 10: click-through outside the cards and Win+D both work.
Acrylic needs Windows 11 22H2+; on Windows 10 the cards use the fallback tint.
Not yet checked on Windows 11.
