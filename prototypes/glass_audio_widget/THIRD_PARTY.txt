References and assets

Hyalite v0.5.0: https://github.com/VII-Cae/hyalite--liquid-glass
The optical approach references rounded-shape distance fields, bevel normals,
Snell-law source displacement and rim lighting. The Qt GLSL implementation is
not Hyalite's JavaScript/SVG runtime or Apple's renderer.

Qt ShaderEffect: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qml-qtquick-shadereffect.html
Selected Lucian SVG poses and SUIT font live in ../audio_widget_concepts/assets.
Their full licenses are LUCIAN-LICENSE.txt and SUIT-LICENSE.txt in that folder.
